# Zomato Restaurant Analytics – Excel

An Excel project where I analysed **9,500+ Zomato restaurant records** to understand restaurant trends, ratings, pricing, customer engagement, and service availability.

## Project Overview

I cleaned and analysed the restaurant data and built an **interactive Excel dashboard** using PivotTables, PivotCharts, KPIs, and slicers.

The main focus was to understand how restaurants are distributed across countries, how ratings and pricing vary, and how services such as online delivery and table booking relate to restaurant performance.

## What I Worked On

* Cleaned and prepared the raw restaurant data for analysis.
* Created PivotTables and PivotCharts to analyse different business areas.
* Built KPI cards for key restaurant metrics.
* Added slicers for interactive country and year-wise analysis.
* Analysed ratings, votes, pricing, restaurant openings, and service availability.
* Used the findings to identify potential markets and business opportunities.

## Key Findings

* Analysed **9,500+ restaurants** across different countries.
* Restaurants with online delivery had a higher average rating (**3.29 vs 2.75**).
* Restaurants offering table booking had a higher average rating (**3.48 vs 2.81**).
* Online delivery was available at around **26% of restaurants** in the dataset.
* Identified countries with relatively lower competition and stronger ratings as potential expansion markets.
