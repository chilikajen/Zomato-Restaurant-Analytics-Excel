# Zomato-Restaurant-Analytics-Excel
Excel-based restaurant analytics dashboard analysing 9,500+ restaurants across countries, customer ratings, pricing, online delivery, table booking, and market opportunities.

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end **Excel data analytics project** using Zomato restaurant data to identify restaurant trends, customer preferences, pricing patterns, service adoption, and potential market opportunities.

The project transforms raw restaurant data into an **interactive Excel dashboard** using PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, KPIs, and data analysis techniques.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyse restaurant distribution across countries and cities.
* Understand restaurant opening trends over time.
* Analyse customer ratings and voting patterns.
* Compare pricing and price-range distribution.
* Evaluate online delivery and table-booking adoption.
* Identify markets with relatively lower competition and stronger ratings.
* Generate actionable recommendations for expansion, pricing, and customer service.

## 📈 Key Dashboard Insights

* Analysed **9,500+ restaurants** across multiple countries.
* **India accounts for the majority of restaurants** in the dataset.
* Restaurants offering online delivery show a **higher average rating** than those without delivery.
* Table-booking-enabled restaurants also demonstrate **higher average ratings**.
* Identified countries with relatively **lower restaurant competition and stronger average ratings** as potential expansion opportunities.
* Analysed restaurant pricing and service adoption to support business decisions.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Increase adoption of online delivery to improve customer reach and engagement.
* Promote table booking as part of the customer experience.
* Explore markets with lower restaurant concentration and stronger ratings.
* Focus on premium restaurant segments where customer demand and ratings are stronger.
* Use cuisine, pricing, and service-level insights to guide expansion decisions.
